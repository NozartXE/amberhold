AMBERHOLD — a beekeeper tower defense. Mira, keeper of the Amberhold, sets poppy turrets, clover sprinklers, hive cannons, wax walls and the Queen's Bell around the Great Hive and holds it through nine pest flows across three seasons — while the swarm gate moves every flow (east, north, west), so last flow's perfect layout leaks this flow. Between flows she drafts one of three boons from a pool of twelve. On the ninth flow the swarm's queen arrives: Vespa, the Hornet Queen. Win: hold all nine flows. Lose: the hive is drained. Deterministic: a fixed-step seeded simulation that runs headless, with a rehearsal plan the bundled balance tool plays to prove every campaign is beatable (tools/balance.mjs).
